About me

A developer who loves both design and logic.

I enjoy building interfaces that stay full-sized, bold and clean, powered by clear, reliable code under the hood.

I’m Ankit Padhiyar, a web developer who combines front-end skills with a strong foundation in C and Python. I like taking ideas from a blank screen to a complete experience — whether it’s a small utility, a learning project, or a full portfolio.

My workflow usually starts with layout and structure in HTML, styling and 3D-inspired visuals in CSS, and then the interactive logic in JavaScript. I use C and Python to keep my problem-solving sharp and think about performance and algorithms.

Some things that describe the way I work:

  • Full-width layouts that feel modern and immersive.
  • Readable, well-structured code with comments where needed.
  • Subtle 3D motion without sacrificing performance.
  • Always experimenting with new ideas and mini-projects.
